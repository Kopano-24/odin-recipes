Lasagna

Description

A delicious baked pasta dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Minced beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare the meat sauce.
  2. Layer the pasta, sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  3. Bake until the pasta is tender and the cheese is golden.
  4. Allow it to cool slightly before serving.
